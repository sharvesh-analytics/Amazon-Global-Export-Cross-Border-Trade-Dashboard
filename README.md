## 📌 Executive Summary

This Business Intelligence dashboard delivers cross-border trade analytics for Amazon global sellers. It evaluates international revenue streams, shipping cost structures, country-level profit margins, customs tariff impacts, and category-wise export volume.

---

## 💡 Key Business Takeaways

- **🌍 Geographic Revenue Hotspots:** Identified top destination countries driving 70%+ of cross-border net margin.
- **📦 Shipping & Logistics Efficiency:** Analyzed freight costs vs delivery lead times across international transit routes.
- **🏷️ Category Profitability:** Pinpointed high-margin export product lines versus high-return risk items.
- **📊 Tariff & Tax Impact:** Built dynamic DAX scenario modeling to measure gross margin shifts under varying customs duty rates.
